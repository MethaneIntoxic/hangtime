# Hangtime competitor analysis

Checked: 2026-08-20. Sources are official product pages, help centres or first-party app listings.

## Positioning conclusion

Hangtime should own the **decision-to-meet** moment: who is available, which area is fair by public transport, what fits everyone&apos;s dietary/taste/budget profile, which bounded shortlist wins, and where to book. It should not try to become another invitation designer, generic calendar poll, restaurant social feed or booking marketplace.

## Current market comparison

| Product | What it does especially well | Gap Hangtime can own |
|---|---|---|
| [Partiful](https://partiful.com/) | Social event pages, one-link browser RSVP, guest lists, reactions, announcements, date polls and guest questionnaires. | No constraint-based venue ranking or fair-transit group decision. |
| [Doodle](https://doodle.com/en/product/polls/) | Mature availability polls, reminders, deadlines, calendar connections and organizer finalization. | Location is a field rather than a jointly ranked venue choice. |
| [Beli](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beli/id1478375386) | Friend-led restaurant discovery, ranked lists, maps and taste matching. | No event availability, bounded group vote or booking decision workflow. |
| [Chope](https://www.chope.co/home) | Strong Singapore discovery, filters, offers and reservation inventory. | Optimizes an individual booking choice rather than small-group consensus. |
| [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/home) | Collaborative itinerary, maps, routes, budgets and saved reservations. | Trip-scale planning is heavier than a fast local meetup decision. |
| [Grab Dine Out](https://www.grab.com/sg/food/dine-out/) | Singapore restaurant discovery, deals and reservation/ride handoff. | Booking-led individual choice; no availability consensus or transparent fair midpoint. |

## Product priorities

### P0 — make the core loop unmistakable

1. Keep proposed occasion/window → availability → fair area → 3–7 venue shortlist → bounded vote → organizer finalizes with reason → calendar/booking as one continuous flow.
2. Preserve frictionless guest links: no install and minimal/no account for an invitee, while returning users gain value from a maintained profile.
3. Show “why this works for us”: dietary pass/fail, per-person transit estimate, group total including tax/service, provider confidence and ranking tradeoffs.
4. Use **occasion** and **hangout** in the product language. Coffee, brunch, lunch, dinner and drinks remain the current F&B set; later activities require their own provider coverage.

### P1 — improve return use and trust

1. Add response reminders and visible invite/readiness status before adding a full social feed.
2. Label booking availability as live, inferred or unknown; never infer inventory from a link.
3. Ask one-tap post-event questions: went, liked it, price accurate and transit fair.
4. Add reusable circles and “plan again” from a past hangout.

### P2 — monetize without corrupting recommendations

1. Explore booking/referral revenue before paid rank manipulation.
2. If sponsored venues are introduced, label them and preserve an unpromoted organic best-fit result.
3. Expand to activities, parks and events only after an occasion/venue-type abstraction and trustworthy provider coverage exist.

## Measurement

- North star: percentage of started plans finalized within 24 hours; track median time-to-finalize.
- Retention: groups planning again within 30 and 60 days.
- Quality: satisfaction, dietary mismatch reports, per-person transit spread, and estimated-versus-actual spend error.
- Trust: organizer override rate/reasons and sponsored-placement visibility.
